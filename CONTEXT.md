# Comunify

Community collaboration and volunteer network connecting volunteers, organizations, and corporate CSR programs.

## Language

**Volunteer**:
A user who offers time and skills to opportunities.
_Avoid_: member, helper

**Organization**:
A nonprofit, informal group, or company that posts opportunities. Owned by an org admin.
_Avoid_: NGO, group, company

**Opportunity**:
A volunteer need posted by an organization with skills, schedule, capacity, and location.
_Avoid_: event, post, need

**Application**:
A volunteer's request to join an opportunity. Lifecycle: pending → confirmed / declined → completed.
_Avoid_: signup, registration, booking

**VolunteerHours**:
Verified hours logged against a completed application for donor and impact reporting.
_Avoid_: timesheet, hours log, attendance
